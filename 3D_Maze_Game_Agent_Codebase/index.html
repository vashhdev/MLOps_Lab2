3D Maze RL Agent

Watch a reinforcement learning agent learn to escape 3D mazes.

Keybinds

  • Arrow keys – Move agent manually
  • H – Toggle HUD visibility
  • 1 – Top‑down camera
  • 2 – Isometric camera
  • 3 – Closer angled camera
  • 4 – Follow camera (third‑person)
  • Mouse drag – Rotate camera (OrbitControls)
  • Mouse wheel – Zoom in/out
  • Enter / Space – Close this screen

Controls

  • Maze – Choose maze size or random
  • Difficulty – Adjust rewards + maze
  • Bulk Train – Fast training (no visuals)
  • Live train – Watch exploration in real time
  • Play greedy – Run the learned policy
